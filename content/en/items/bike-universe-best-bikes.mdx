## How to Judge Any City Bike Before You Buy

Before you commit to any of the Bike Universe best bikes, run the same five-minute inspection you would apply to any urban bicycle. Marketing copy tells you how a bike is positioned; these checks tell you how it will actually behave on wet pavement at 7 a.m.

| What to check | Why it matters | Quick test |
|---|---|---|
| Standover height | You need clearance when you stop suddenly | Straddle the frame with both feet flat |
| Reach and saddle position | Poor fit causes wrist and back pain | Ride 5 minutes; adjust before deciding |
| Brake type and condition | Wet-weather stopping power is non-negotiable | Squeeze hard; levers should not hit the bar |
| Tire width and clearance | Wider tires plus fenders equal comfort | Confirm room for fenders if you commute |
| Mounts for rack and fenders | Carrying capacity beats a sweaty back | Look for eyelets on frame and fork |
| Gearing range | Steep routes demand lower gears | Ask for the lowest and highest gear ratio |

If you cannot test ride in person, ask the seller directly about frame size, standover height, and whether returns are accepted. A short email exchange before purchase is far cheaper than shipping a bike back.

## Smart Buying Tips for the Bike Universe Best Bikes

The sticker price is only part of the total cost of commuting. Plan for accessories in the same budget conversation, because a bike without a lock, lights, and a helmet is not yet a commuter — it is a project.

- **Budget for gear up front.** A lock, front and rear lights, a helmet, and fenders are essentials, not upgrades.

## Keeping Your New Bike Running

The Bike Universe best bikes will reward a little routine attention. Most premature wear on commuter bikes comes from two things: underinflated tires and a dry chain. Both take minutes to fix and cost almost nothing to prevent.

| Interval | Task | Time needed |
|---|---|---|
| Every ride | Check tire pressure by feel, spin the wheels | 1 minute |
| Weekly | Wipe and lube the chain, check brake pads | 10 minutes |
| Monthly | Inspect bolts, cables, and tire tread wear | 20 minutes |
| Seasonally | Clean drivetrain, check bearing play | 45 minutes |
| Annually | Full professional tune-up | Shop visit |

Store your bike indoors or under cover where possible. Rain and road salt are the quiet killers of chains, cables, and bearings, and a dry, covered bike lasts noticeably longer between services.
