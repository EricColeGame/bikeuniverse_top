## Habits That Make Bike Universe Bike Unlocks Faster

The difference between a fast garage and a slow one is usually a handful of habits, not skill.

- **Bank before you browse.** Window shopping with a full wallet invites impulse buys you'll regret by phase 3.
- **Test before you commit.** If a bike can be test-ridden, ride it on your worst route, not a straight highway.
- **Learn two shortcuts per area.** Map knowledge is free speed, and free speed is free currency.
- **Read patch notes.** During development, unlock requirements can change between builds. What was a grind last week may be a gift this week.
- **Ignore rarity until it's cheap.** Rarity is a collector stat. Speed, handling, and acceleration are earning stats.

Here are the mistakes that quietly cost players the most progress:

| Mistake | Why it hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Buying the rarest affordable bike | Rarity doesn't reduce travel time | Buy the best all-rounder instead |
