## How to Spot Fake Bike Universe Codes

Fake code lists are everywhere, and early-development games attract them because nobody can prove the codes don't exist. Use these filters:

- **No source link.** A code with no screenshot, no post, and no timestamp is a guess.
- **"Generator" tools.** Anything asking for your Roblox login to "generate" codes is a scam, full stop.
- **Codes posted by accounts with no history.** Check whether the account has ever discussed the game before.
- **Rewards that are too generous.** "10,000 cash and a free premium bike" during a test build is fantasy.
- **Copy-paste walls of 30+ codes.** Real drops are usually one to five codes at a time.

The safest rule: if a code can't be traced to an official channel or a verified community post with a date, don't type it anywhere.
